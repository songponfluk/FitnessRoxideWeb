Privacy Policy · Last updated 12 November 2026

Summary

ROXIDE does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

Your profile (name, division, age group, country), race results and language preference are saved only in the app's local storage on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to any third party. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Photos

If you tap "Save to Photos", ROXIDE asks for add-only access so it can save your result card. The app cannot read your photo library.

Analytics, ads and tracking

ROXIDE contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

ROXIDE does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds online features (such as accounts or leaderboards), this policy will be updated before that version is released and the app will ask for your consent where required.
